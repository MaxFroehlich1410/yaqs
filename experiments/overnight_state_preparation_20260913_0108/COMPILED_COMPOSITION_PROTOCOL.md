# Known-input compilation before structural optimization — development recipe

This is a new composed candidate, motivated by the complete 24-pair state-preparation compiler diagnostic. It does not retroactively modify the earlier composition or the original failed MPS parameter gate. The earlier composition passed its primary comparison against all seven roles at the four-structural cutoff (+0.720699 percentage points, four of four target wins); its all-six-trained minimum-time sensitivity comparison (+0.477946 percentage points) missed the threshold. No development science starts until this protocol, kernel, driver and tests pass the coordinator's full lint/freeze. No confirmation or q8 target is loaded by this development driver.

Use all four fixed q6 development targets at replica11. Authenticate the completed initializer-policy conditional endpoints, including exact physical gate metadata, source arrays and known complete declared initializer costs. Those endpoints already received the old generic compiler. This development experiment reuses their arrays read-only; its reused construction cost is a procedure charge, not fresh physical construction. A future parent may supply an independently authenticated lazy initializer through the public execute API.

Pipeline for every one of the seven roles:

1. The supplied generic-compiled conditional initializer is available at refinement time zero.
2. Run the frozen zero-input state-preparation compiler once physically. Every role is charged its complete compiler and independent state-audit CPU. The resulting common circuit/theta is the sole training start. Its retained physical gate metadata and compact independent parameter mapping are preserved.
3. Run the identical six existing training roles with unchanged numerical primitives and configurations. No intermediate compiler is introduced and no noisy score selects a compiler output.
4. At the final raw iterate, run the old generic compiler, then the same zero-input state-preparation compiler. Expose the raw final point immediately, the generic result only after its compiler cost, and the state-preparation result only after its further cost.

The unchanged role receives both final compiler opportunities too. Repeated identical compiler results may be physically cached once, with the same complete compiler cost still charged to each declared standalone procedure. All compiler attempts, independent ideal-state audits and failed executions remain in the physical ledger.

The primary four roles are structural mean2 L-BFGS, exact Adam, weighted Adam and adaptive online. Additional trained controls are fixed mean2 L-BFGS and fixed noiseless Adam. The unchanged reference is the seventh role. The equal mean of depolarizing_1s_all and dephasing_1s_all is unchanged. Exact structural methods retain 3000 individual-channel calls (1500 joint calls), warmup150 joint calls, repair25 joint calls, shortlist2, at most15 rounds and protected0. Stochastic methods retain1500 total updates, the existing weighted trajectory allocation and repair/warmup rules. Fixed noiseless Adam retains the existing1500-update ablation. Repaired branches retain their frozen primitive's memory policy: exact branches start fresh moments or curvature, while stochastic branches preserve and project their existing optimizer state as specified by the unchanged sampler driver.

The initial compiler cost is added once as an offset to each role's refinement curve. It is not also added to the supplied initializer_cpu_seconds field. Total CPU availability is supplied initializer CPU plus refinement CPU. The common initial offset cancels between matched roles, while remaining included in their full procedure charges. All unsuccessful/rejected branches, line searches and caught interruptions remain charged.

Primary comparison: take the minimum completed refinement CPU of the four structural roles, then use common_control_audit.select_points to select the last chronologically available point for all six trained roles and unchanged at that exact same cutoff. A shorter completed control retains its last available point. Also report all seven roles at the minimum of the six trained final times as a sensitivity analysis, plus all final endpoints. Missing/failed roles make the corresponding comparison unavailable; never drop them and pool a smaller panel.

All selected and final physical endpoints receive the complete unchanged all12 exact benchmark evaluation, with explicitly ordered noise IDs and no endpoint selection by fidelity. Fresh training streams use namespace compiled_composition_training, target identity and replica11. Exact final density evaluations use no training randomness. Compilers inspect only the ideal zero-input preparation and retain the independently audited global phase; they do not claim noisy equivalence.

Fixed B* is structural_mean2_weighted_adam. The candidate remains structural_mean2_lbfgs. Promotion requires at least +0.5 percentage points in equal-target all11 noisy mean and wins on all four targets at the primary common cutoff against both fixed B* and the strongest aggregate control among all other six roles. A weaker control cannot be substituted after seeing results. Otherwise this new recipe fails its development screen and the old candidate remains the reference; no tuning grid or confirmation launch follows automatically.

Public kernel execute(root, source, target, exact_config, stochastic_config, seed, order=TRAINED) accepts supplied arrays and never loads targets. Full curves are saved once per arm, with separate compact training receipts and original frozen primitive checkpoints. Outer EXECUTION/RESULT records contain arm references, scores, point selections, costs and complete artifact hashes, without copied curves. Cached public theta, cost, score and selection fields are reconstructed from compiler/training/score child receipts. Re-signing an edited summary alone cannot authorize it. Numerical failures are retained as failed arm receipts; catchable interruption resumes durable work, and an unknown running case/operation marker blocks automatic resume.

The outer case attempt measures initial compilation, training, all compiler passes, scoring, bookkeeping and checkpoint I/O. Nested timers are not additive. Input/source verification and final outer record serialization remain separately excluded engineering overhead. The coordinator allocates at most two single-thread development workers after freeze. No settings are configurable through the scientific CLI; reduced q2 configurations exist only in tests.
