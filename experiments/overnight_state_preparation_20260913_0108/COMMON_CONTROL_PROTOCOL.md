# Additional controls at the structural CPU cutoff

This is a development diagnostic declared after the complete composition panel
and before confirmation. It cannot retrospectively alter the original gate.
All four original development targets and replica 11 are retained. There is no
new training, compiler pass, candidate selection or target omission.

Keep the exact primary cutoff already defined by the minimum completed
refinement CPU among the four structural optimizers, with the common measured
initializer cost added. At that cutoff, select the latest chronological actual
checkpoint of every trained control, including fixed mean-two L-BFGS and fixed
noiseless Adam. A shorter completed arm remains available at its final circuit;
a final compilation whose time is later than the cutoff remains unavailable.
No interpolation, quality-based checkpoint selection, or retroactive compilation
is allowed. The original four structural points and scores must remain identical.

Before any new scores, freeze all selected circuit/parameter points and source
case digests in PROTOCOL.json. Authenticate the old complete source protocol,
summary and every case dependency. Score the two extra controls under all twelve
fixed benchmark conditions in a separate new output directory. Keep all old
artifacts unchanged. Reused scores are references, not newly charged numerical
work. Exact expectations need no Monte Carlo confidence interval.

The diagnostic asks whether weighted Adam remains the strongest aggregate
control at the original primary cutoff. It reports every control, the strongest
aggregate control over the complete target panel, all per-target differences,
and whether the original candidate's >=0.5 percentage-point, four-positive-sign
development criterion would also hold against that strongest control. It does
not choose a different CPU cutoff or change the method after seeing these scores.

The inclusive measured operation covers fresh control evaluation, circuit-bank
writes and retained catchable retries. All12 operation timers are nested and
must not be added to that inclusive operation. Target loading, source custody,
store setup, read-only resume verification and final reporting are engineering
overhead outside this timer. A durable running marker blocks unknown-kill resume.
The original initializer and training costs are reused only for the comparison
clock, never counted again as physical work.
